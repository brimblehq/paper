---
title: "Build minutes"
description: "How Brimble tracks included build minutes, purchased top-ups, and monthly resets."
---

Build minutes are a separate balance inside Brimble billing.

Your available build minutes come from two sources:

1. The minutes included with your current plan for the active billing cycle.
2. Extra purchased build minutes that are added on top.

## Included minutes vs purchased minutes

Brimble stores purchased build minutes separately from your plan's included allowance.

That matters because:

- Included minutes reset with the billing cycle.
- Purchased minutes carry forward until you use them.

On paid subscriptions, the reset date follows the subscription's Stripe billing cycle. On free accounts, build minutes are reset by Brimble's monthly free-user reset job.

## How top-ups work

Build-minute top-ups are one-off purchases.

Today, the billing service applies these rules:

- The minimum purchase amount is **$5**.
- A successful top-up credits minutes immediately.
- The credited minutes are added both to your current visible balance and to your stored extra-minute balance for future cycles.

By default, Brimble credits **288 build minutes per $1**. At that rate:

- `$5` adds `1,440` minutes
- `$10` adds `2,880` minutes

## What happens at renewal

When a paid subscription renews successfully, Brimble resets the build-minute balance for the new cycle to:

```text
plan-included minutes + any extra purchased minutes
```

This prevents purchased top-ups from being wiped out when the monthly allowance refreshes.

## What happens when you change plan

When you move between personal plans, Brimble adjusts your included build-minute baseline to match the new plan.

The extra purchased minutes stay attached to your account. They are not discarded during an upgrade, downgrade, or trial start.

The same idea applies to Team workspaces: the team workspace keeps its own build-minute balance, and team top-ups are credited to that team workspace rather than to your personal workspace.

## When build minutes can still be unavailable

Running out of build minutes is not the only reason builds can stop.

Builds can also be disabled when:

- Your subscription becomes unpaid.
- A spending limit reaches its configured pause threshold.

If that happens, adding more build minutes alone may not restore builds. You may also need to pay the open invoice or raise the spending limit.

## Related billing behavior

Build-minute purchases are blocked in one important case: if you try to buy build minutes for a subscription that is already marked **unpaid**, Brimble rejects the purchase until the open invoice is settled.

This prevents top-ups from being used as a workaround for a subscription debt state.

## Where build minutes fit in billing

Build minutes sit alongside, not inside, your metered infrastructure charges.

That means:

- Your plan subscription is still billed on its normal cycle.
- Usage-based charges such as CPU, memory, bandwidth, and storage still appear in the billing estimate.
- Build-minute top-ups are separate one-off transactions.
